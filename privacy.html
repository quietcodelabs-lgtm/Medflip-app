Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Quiet Code Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MedFlip.

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your name, email address, medical specialty, and any preferences you set within the app. We also collect usage data (such as articles saved and time spent reading) to power features like "Reading DNA".

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve the MedFlip app, to personalize your experience (like suggesting relevant medical abstracts), and to communicate with you about updates or support.

3. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with third-party service providers (like analytics or hosting platforms) only to the extent necessary to provide our services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at quietcodelabs@gmail.com.
